CONFIDENTIAL — TARGET DOSSIER
Company: Solenne AI
Stage: Series A
Sector: LLM Infrastructure — Agent Memory & Retrieval

=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
Solenne AI builds a low-latency vector search and reasoning-cache layer purpose-built
for LLM agent memory. The core product is a proprietary distributed indexing engine
that lets multi-agent systems recall relevant context in under 40ms at scale, without
the retrieval-latency penalties of standard vector DB wrappers.

=== ENGINEERING & PRODUCT ===
- 14-person engineering team, several ex-Google Research and DeepMind alumni.
- Proprietary distributed indexing algorithm; provisional patent filed (not yet granted).
- Sub-40ms p99 retrieval latency at 10M+ vector scale, independently benchmarked by
  two of the six pilot customers.
- 11,400 GitHub stars on the open-source client SDK; strong inbound developer interest.
- Currently 6 paying enterprise pilots; GA release is not yet public.
- Two pilots have technically integrated the full stack into production agent workflows;
  the remaining four are still in evaluation/sandbox mode.
- Engineering NPS among hands-on developer users is very high (unprompted praise for
  API ergonomics and latency in support channels).
- No dedicated DevRel or enterprise sales engineering function yet — technical
  onboarding for new pilots is handled directly by founding engineers.

=== FINANCIALS ===
