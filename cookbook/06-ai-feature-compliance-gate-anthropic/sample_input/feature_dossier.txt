FEATURE LAUNCH DOSSIER — "CarePredict AI Triage Assistant"

Overview:
A new AI-powered symptom-triage assistant for a European telehealth platform.
Patients describe symptoms in free text; the model suggests a likely urgency
level (self-care / GP visit / urgent care) and recommends next steps.

Data & Storage:
- Collects free-text symptom descriptions, age, sex, and prior-condition flags.
- Stores full conversation transcripts and structured health flags in a managed
  Postgres instance hosted in us-east-1 (AWS).
- Analytics events (including symptom keywords) are streamed to a third-party
  product-analytics vendor based in the United States.
- Transcripts are retained indefinitely for "model improvement".

Model & Automation:
- The triage suggestion is shown directly to the patient with no clinician in
  the loop before display.
- A confidence score gates whether the "urgent care" banner is shown.

Tech Stack:
- Inference served via an open-source model fine-tuned in-house.
- The fine-tuning pipeline links against an AGPL-3.0 licensed data-processing
  library, and the whole service is distributed as a SaaS product.

Timeline:
- Targeting EU (Germany, France) launch in 6 weeks.
